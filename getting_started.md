## 🎲 The Game on One Page

| | |
| :--- | :--- |
| 🎭 **Who you play** | A crew from a poor district in the Works. Your gang is under contract to **Antioch**, a noble house. Antioch keeps the water running, and the crew does its dirty work |
| 💼 **What you do** | Take **jobs** from Antioch's **handler**: break-ins, sabotage, kidnappings. Don't get traced back to Antioch |
| 🏠 **Why you do it** | Your **community**. The pay comes home, and the crew decides how much goes to the district and how much it keeps |
| 🎲 **How you roll** | **2D6, 7 or more to pass.** Traits, gear and good positioning add dice. Danger takes them away. You always keep two |
| 📈 **How you grow** | Jobs earn **Marks**. Every 4 Marks is a level, with new traits, talents, contacts and markets |
| ⚖️ **What's at stake** | **Exposure** (how close you are to being traced), **Trust** (how much the handler will do for you) and **Community** (what's left at home) |

### 🔁 The loop

```
 📜 Briefing ─▶ 💼 The job ─▶ 🪙 Homecoming ─▶ 🌙 Downtime ─┐
      ▲                                                     │
      └─────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘
```

Each time round the loop is one job. A session usually covers one job and its Homecoming. The details are in [Community: the Campaign Loop](community.md#-the-campaign-loop).

## 🌆 The World in Brief

Just enough of the setting to read the rules. The *Project Society — World Bible* is the authority on everything else.

| Term | What it means in play |
| :--- | :--- |
| 🏙️ **Allaneus** | The setting. Its currency is the **chip** |
| ✨ **The Heights** | The top of society, home of the noble **houses**. Bribes cost 40c, and failing one gets the crew reported |
| 🎩 **The Terraces** | Below the Heights. Servants of the houses, and the well-off. The **Courtier** trait works here |
| 🤝 **The Margin** | Where the upper and lower layers meet and trade. Fixers work here |
| 🔩 **The Works** | The industrial lower layer: foundries, boilers and pressure lines. The crew's home |
| 🛶 **The Sunk** | The underworld beneath the Works: smugglers, fences, lenders and the Refined black market |
| 💎 **Refinement**, **Refined** | Upper-class technology, often grown rather than built. Illegal for Works folk to carry |
| 🏛️ **The houses** | The noble families. Rival houses run crews of their own |
| 💧 **Antioch** | The house the gang works for. It controls the district's water, and its elite end raids |
| 📜 **The contract** | Work for Antioch in return for water and protection. A crew that's **Burned** loses both |
| 🎖️ **The Liveried** | Antioch's uniformed household. The **handler** is the crew's contact with them, and Livery opens Refined doors |
| 🏚️ **The gang** and **the district** | The crew's people and their home in the Works. The district's strength is its **Community** score |

> 🔒 **GM only.** The handler has a secret. It's in [Exposure & Trust](exposure_and_trust.md#-gm-only-the-handlers-secret) and the World Bible.

## 🪑 Session Zero

Before the first job, spend twenty minutes agreeing on how the table plays.

### 🛡️ Safety

The game is about poverty, loyalty and being used by the powerful. Jobs can involve **violence, kidnapping, sickness, debt and death**, and the [Hardship](community.md#-hardship-events) table can hurt the people your characters love.

| Tool | How it works |
| :--- | :--- |
| ⛔ **Lines** | Things that never appear in the game. Anyone can name one, and nobody asks why |
| 🌫️ **Veils** | Things that can happen, but off screen. "The interrogation happens. Cut to the next morning." |
| ✋ **Pause** | Anyone can say "pause" (or tap a card) at any time. The scene stops, changes or skips. No explanation is needed |
| 💬 **Check in** | After a heavy scene, the DM asks the table whether everyone's all right to carry on |

Write the lines and veils down where everyone can see them, and add to them at any time.

### 🤝 Expectations

Talk about these now, so they don't become arguments later:

- **Characters can die.** A failed survival roll is death. Is everyone happy with that?
- **The Pot.** How will the crew decide what to send home? Unanimous, majority, or each character's own chips? The [rules](community.md#-homecoming) leave it to the table, and the argument is part of the game, so agree on *how* to argue.
- **Player view.** If players use the app, they should switch on **🙈 Player view** in the Rules tab to hide GM-only sections.

## 🆕 The First Session

| Step | What to do | Rules |
| :---: | :--- | :--- |
| **1** | 🧰 **Make characters.** About ten minutes each. Make sure the crew can cover fighting, sneaking, talking and patching people up | [Character Creation](character_creation.md) |
| **2** | 🏠 **Set up the crew sheet.** Name the district. Community **3** (Clinic I, Hall I, and Tier I of one more project, chosen by the crew). Need **10c**, Pot **0c**, Exposure **0**, Trust **3**, Water **on** | [Community](community.md#-the-community-track) |

## 💼 Running a Job

> ⚠️ **GM reference.** This section is advice for the DM, not new rules. The 🟡 proposals are in [Design Decisions](#-design-decisions).

### 📜 The briefing

Give the crew four things:

1. **The objective**, in one sentence. *"Get the shipping ledger out of the Varro counting house before the audit."*
2. **The pay**, set by the risk: 🟢 **Simple** 30c, 🟡 **Standard** 60c, 🔴 **Dangerous** 120c ([job pay](equipment.md#-job-pay)).
3. **What the handler knows.** At Trust 0–2, leave one important detail out ([Trust bands](exposure_and_trust.md#trust-bands)).
4. **Favours.** The crew asks for them now ([Favours](exposure_and_trust.md#-favours)).

### 🧩 The shape of a job

Most jobs are four or five scenes:

| Scene | Ask yourself | Typical rolls |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 🚪 **The way in** | How do they reach the place unseen? | Shadow, Courtier, Forger, Streetwise |
| 🧱 **The obstacle** | What stands between them and the objective? A lock, a guard post, a ledger in code | Thief, Smooth Talker, Lettered, Mechanic |
| 🎯 **The objective** | What makes the moment tense? A clock, a witness, a twist | Whatever fits |
| 🏃 **The way out** | What has changed since they came in? | Runner, Driver, a [chase](actions.md#-chases), a fight |
| 🪙 **Homecoming** | Count Exposure, pay and Marks | [Wrapping up](#-wrapping-up) |

Give every obstacle **at least two ways through**. Traits overlap on purpose ([Character Creation](character_creation.md#-trait-list)), so a locked door can be picked, forced, talked past or climbed around.

### 🎚️ Difficulty and when to roll

- **Roll only if failure is possible and interesting** ([Out of Combat](actions.md#-out-of-combat)).
- **Easy** (`+1`) or **Hard** (`-1`): use one, or neither. The opposition sets it ([Who rolls](actions.md#-who-rolls)).
- **Specialist work** needs a covering trait. Say so **before** the roll.
- **Darkness, hazards and chases** have their own rules in [Actions](actions.md#-darkness).

### 💥 When a roll fails

A failed roll should **move the story**, not stop it. Pick one cost:

| Cost | Example |
| :--- | :--- |
| 👁️ **Exposure** | Someone saw: `+1` ([Gaining Exposure](exposure_and_trust.md#-gaining-exposure)) |
| ⏱️ **Time** | It works, but the guards' round comes by. The next scene starts badly |
| 🎒 **Cost** | A tool snaps, a bribe is lost, a consumable is used up |
| 🩸 **Harm** | A [hazard](actions.md#-hazards) roll: the fall, the scald, the dog |
| 🧱 **Position** | The next test is **Hard**, because the door is barred now |

If nothing interesting can come of a failure, don't call for a roll.

### 👥 Opposition

Rough guide for a crew of **four**. Add or remove one enemy for each crew member more or fewer. Profiles are in [Enemies](actions.md#-enemies).

| Job | A typical fight |
| :--- | :--- |
| 🟢 **Simple** | 4–6 **raiders**, or 2 **gang toughs** |
| 🟡 **Standard** | 3–4 **house guards** or **gang toughs**, plus a **guard dog** |
| 🔴 **Dangerous** | As Standard, plus a **rival crew agent** or an **Antioch elite** |

**One fight per job is plenty**, or two on a Dangerous job. Every fight risks Exposure, and the crew should always have a quieter way to win.

### 🧾 Wrapping up

At the end of the job, in order:

1. **Objective:** completed or failed? Failure means **half pay**.
2. **Exposure gained:** 0 or 1 makes it a [clean job](exposure_and_trust.md#-clean-jobs) (+10c, +1 Trust, +1 Mark).
3. **Trust:** +1 for success, −1 for failure, and any other [Trust events](exposure_and_trust.md#gaining-and-losing-trust).
4. **Pay:** the job's pay, ±10c for clean or Hot.
5. **Marks:** for everyone on the job ([Marks](progression.md#-marks)).
6. Go to [Homecoming](community.md#-homecoming).

The app's **🏠 Crew** tab does all of this when you end a job.

## 📋 Quick Reference

| | |
| :--- | :--- |
| 🎲 **Roll** | 2D6, **7+** passes. Double 6 is a **critical**. Double 1 is a **fumble** (in combat, your turn ends) |
| ⚖️ **Modifiers** | Each `+dice` or `-dice` adds a die. **Cancel**, then **cap at `-3`**. Keep the highest two (net `+`) or the lowest two (net `-`) |
| ⬆️ **Common `+1`** | Trait (one per roll) · tool (one per roll) · helper (one) · Easy · Aim · weapon (`+1`/`+2`, To Wound only) |
| ⬇️ **Common `-1`** | Hard · no trait but it needs know-how · long range · cover or darkness · smoke · armour (`-1`/`-2`, To Wound) · each Shaken |
| 🎬 **A turn** | One **Move** (6") and one **Action**. Crew and enemies take turns activating one model each |
| 🩸 **Injury Table** | Wound roll: **2–6** Shaken · **7–11** 1 wound · **12** 3 wounds and a free attack |
| 🫀 **Dying** | Last wound box filled. After the fight, a survival roll (a Medic test). Fail and the character dies |
| 🪙 **Homecoming** | Need → Hardship → Raids → Water → Pot → Build → Split |
| 🌙 **Downtime** | Recovery → one downtime action each → level up → shop → Exposure −1 |

## 📖 Glossary

| Term | Meaning |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Advance** | The reward for each level from 2 to 10: a trait, a talent or a contact |
| **Burned** | Exposure 10. Antioch cuts the crew loose |
| **Chips (c)** | The currency |
| **Clean job** | A job that gained 0 or 1 Exposure |
| **Community** | The district's strength, 0–10. It always equals the number of project tiers built |
| **Crew level** | The level most of the crew is at (the higher one, if it's split evenly) |
| **Downtime** | Recovery, actions, levelling and shopping between jobs |
| **Dying** | Every wound box is filled. A survival roll decides what happens |
| **Exposure** | 0–10. How close the crew is to being traced back to Antioch |
| **Favour** | Help from the handler, asked for at the briefing. Trust decides how many |
| **Hardship test** | The district's roll when the Need isn't paid in full |
| **Homecoming** | The seven steps after every job, where the pay comes home |
| **Marks** | Experience. Every 4 is a level |
| **Need** | The chips the district must have after every job |
| **Pot** | Chips saved by the district to build project tiers |
| **Raid check / Raid test** | Is the district raided? If it is, does it hold? |
| **Scar** | A lasting mark from nearly dying. Story only |
| **Shaken** | `-1 dice` on every roll until the end of combat. Stacks to `-3`, and more becomes wounds |
| **Test** | Any roll that isn't an attack |
| **Tier** | One level of a project (Clinic I, II, III). Each is one point of Community |
| **Trust** | 0–10. The handler's opinion of the crew |

The dice terms are in the [Dice System glossary](dice_system.md#-glossary).
