## 🏙️ Allaneus at a Glance

Allaneus is a city built in layers. The noble **houses** live at the top, in the Heights, surrounded by **Refinement**: technology that's grown rather than built. Their servants and the well-off live below them in the Terraces. At the bottom are **the Works**, the foundries, boilers and pressure lines that keep the whole city running, and the people who work them. Under the Works is **the Sunk**, where anything can be bought.

The houses don't share power and don't share water. Each Works district depends on a house for its water and its protection, and pays for both with loyalty, labour and, when the house asks, deniable work.

## 🧱 The Layers of the City

| Layer | Who lives there | What it feels like |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| ✨ **The Heights** | The noble houses and their households | Quiet, clean and grown. Doors open only for those who belong |
| 🎩 **The Terraces** | Servants of the houses, and the well-off | Respectable, watchful, and always looking up |
| 🤝 **The Margin** | Fixers, traders and go-betweens | Where the upper and lower layers meet and do business |
| 🔩 **The Works** | Labourers, gangs and their families | Heat, noise and steam. Home |
| 🛶 **The Sunk** | Smugglers, fences, lenders and worse | The underworld beneath the Works, and the only place to buy stolen Refinement |

### ✨ The Heights

The top of society, and home of the noble houses. Refinement is everywhere: Refined locks that no Works tool can open, and guards who notice anyone who doesn't belong. A bribe here costs more than a month's wages in the Works, and an official who turns one down reports whoever offered it.

### 🎩 The Terraces

Below the Heights live the people who serve the houses: stewards, clerks, footmen, tutors and the merchants rich enough to sit close to power. A good suit of clothes and the right manners get you through the Terraces. Conspicuous gear and Works accents get you noticed.

<!-- Prompts: Who runs the Terraces day to day? Are there Terraces families with their own ambitions?

### 🤝 The Margin

The Margin is where the upper and lower layers meet and trade. Fixers work here, finding buyers and making introductions between people who would never be seen together. It's the one place where a Works face and a Terraces coat can share a table.

<!-- Prompts: Is the Margin a place (a market, a bridge, a street) or a kind of business?

### 🔩 The Works

The industrial lower layer: foundries, boilers, pump houses and miles of pressure lines. It's divided into **districts**, each with its own gang and each tied to a house for its water. Your crew grew up here.

<!-- Prompts: How many districts are there? Do they have names? What does the Works make, and who for?
     What's a shift like? What's the weather like under all that steam? -->

### 🛶 The Sunk

Beneath the Works lies the Sunk: old channels, flooded cellars and the people who live in them. Smugglers move goods through it, fences sell what can't be sold upstairs, lenders cover a district's debts at a price, and cleaners make problems disappear. Stolen Refinement can only be bought and sold here.

## 💎 Refinement

Refinement is the technology of the upper classes, and much of it is **grown** rather than built. Refined things look like fine clothing, glass and polished brass, and they work better than anything the Works can make: suits that turn a blade, coats that mend their wearer, keys that open any lock.

- **It's illegal for Works folk.** Being caught with Refined gear is serious trouble, and a Refined **graft** grown into the body is worse.
- **It opens doors.** Refined locks and doors can only be opened with Refined tools, or by someone wearing a house's Livery.
- **It blends in upstairs.** In the Heights and Terraces, Refined gear draws no attention. Works gear does.
- **The Works can't make it.** Even the brass alloy in a chip can't be made in a Works foundry.

## 🏛️ The Houses

The noble houses rule Allaneus from the Heights. Each has its own household, its own interests, and its own districts in the Works that depend on it. The houses are rivals, but they rarely fight in the open. Instead they hire **crews** from the Works to do the work they can't be seen doing: break-ins, sabotage, theft, kidnapping. If a crew is caught, the house has never heard of them.

A house that's caught using a crew loses face, and that matters more in the Heights than almost anything else.

## 💧 House Antioch and the Contract

**Antioch** is the house your gang works for. It controls your district's water.

- **The contract.** The gang does Antioch's work, and in return the water keeps running and the district is protected. It's not a choice anyone made freely. A district without water doesn't last long.
- **The Liveried.** Antioch's uniformed household. Their Livery opens Refined doors and upper gates.
- **The handler.** Your crew's contact with the Liveried. The handler gives you jobs, pays you, and decides how much to tell you. Earn their trust and they'll lend you gear and favours. Lose it and the briefings get thinner.
- **The elite.** When raiders hit the district, Antioch's elite come and end it. They're always late, but they always come, as long as the crew hasn't been cut loose.
- **Burned.** A crew that's traced back to Antioch is Burned. No more jobs, no more protection, and the water may be cut until the crew earns its way back.

## 🏚️ Life in the Works

Each district is a few streets of tenements, workshops and pump houses, held together by its gang and the people who depend on it. When a district does well, repairs get done and kids go to the Hall instead of the street. When it does badly, the empty shopfronts spread and families leave for other gangs.

What a district builds says what it cares about:

| Place | What it is |
| :--- | :--- |
| 🩺 **The Clinic** | Often a corner of the pump house, kept alive on donations and stubbornness |
| 🔧 **The Workshop** | Where things get fixed, made and sold on |
| 👁️ **The Watch** | Lookouts and bells for when raiders come |
| 💧 **The Cistern** | Water the house can't cut off |
| 🏚️ **The Safehouse** | Somewhere to disappear for a while |
| 📚 **The Hall** | Where the district meets, and where children learn |

**The pump house** matters more than anything. When the pressure drops, the whole district feels it.

### Trouble at home

- **Raiders** from other districts come for whatever they can carry.
- **Sickness** goes through a district fast when the water's bad.
- **Debt** to Sunk lenders always comes back doubled.
- **Unrest** starts with arguments over the ration board and ends with it burning.

## 🥊 Gangs, Crews and Rivals

Every Works district has a **gang**: part protection, part family, part business. The gang keeps order, collects what's owed, and holds the district's contract with its house. A **crew** is the handful of gang members who do the house's deniable work.

| Who | What the Works knows about them |
| :--- | :--- |
| 🐀 **Raiders** | Desperate people from other districts who hit what they can see and run before the house's elite arrive |
| 🥊 **Rival gangs** | Other districts' gangs, with their own houses and their own muscle. Some trade with you. Some want your streets |
| 🗡️ **Rival crews** | Other houses' crews. Better paid, better armed, and sometimes carrying Refined weapons |
| 💂 **House guards** | Armoured, watchful, and quick to raise the alarm |
| 🤝 **Fixers** | Margin go-betweens who find buyers and make introductions |
| 🏷️ **Fences** | Sunk traders who move goods with no questions |

## 🪙 Chips and Trade

The currency of Allaneus is the **chip**: a small token of Refined brass alloy. The alloy can't be made in the Works, so chips can't be forged by hand. Every chip that comes down from the Heights is a little piece of Refinement in a Works pocket.

Better goods are harder to find. Everyday gear is sold on any Works street. Specialist gear comes from gang armourers and Margin dealers. Refined goods are stolen, and only fences in the Sunk will sell them. Prices are in the [Market](equipment.md#-markets).

## 💬 Street Talk

Words you'll hear in the Works.

| Word | Meaning |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Upstairs** | The Terraces and the Heights |
| **Refined** | Upper-class technology, or anything made of it |
| **Livery** | A house's uniform. Wearing it opens doors |
| **The handler** | The crew's contact in a house's household |
| **Chips** | Money |
| **Burned** | Traced back to your house and cut loose |
| **The Pot** | What the district saves to build with |
| **The Need** | What the district must have after every job, just to get by |
