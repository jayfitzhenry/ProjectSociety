## 🗂️ Adding History

- **Format.** The history arrives as markdown and replaces this page. If it grows too long for one page, split it into files named `world_<topic>.md` (for example `world_houses.md`) and add each one to the **World** group in `js/data.js`. The app then shows it under **World** in the Rules tab, in search and in the topic index.
- **Audience.** All of it is for players. Nothing here is GM only, so GM secrets such as the handler's secret stay out of it.
- **Source.** The *Project Society — World Bible* shapes it. The first draft's structure is worked out from the World Bible, then filled in.
