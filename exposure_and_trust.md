# 👁️ Project Society — Exposure & Trust

> **Exposure** is how close the crew's work comes to being traced back to Antioch.
> **Trust** is how much the handler is willing to do for the crew.
> Keep the first low and the second high, and Antioch keeps the water running.

See also [Community](community.md), [Actions: Downtime](actions.md#-downtime), [Equipment](equipment.md) and [Progression](progression.md).

## 🔦 Exposure

**Exposure** is a number from **0 to 10** on the crew sheet. It belongs to the whole crew, and everyone can see it. A new campaign starts at **0**.

Antioch must never be seen backing a gang. Every witness, alarm and body left behind is a thread someone at court could pull. If too many threads lead to Antioch, Antioch cuts the crew loose.

---

## 📈 Gaining Exposure

The DM adds Exposure **as it happens**, and says so out loud.

| Event | Exposure |
| :--- | :---: |
| 👀 Seen by a witness who could describe the crew | `+1` |
| 🚨 An alarm is raised | `+1` |
| 📢 Using **Conspicuous** gear openly in the Terraces or Heights (once per job) | `+1` |
| 🩸 A body or obvious evidence left behind | `+1` each |
| 💸 A failed **Heights bribe** | `+1` |
| 🪪 Identified by name or face, or linked to Antioch | `+2` |
| ✨ Caught with **Refined** gear or a graft | `+2` |
| 🏃 A rival house's crew sees you and gets away | `+2` |

**Silent** weapons don't raise alarms or add Exposure for noise ([Equipment](equipment.md#special-rules)).

### ✨ Clean jobs

If the crew gains **0 or 1 Exposure** during a job, it's a **clean job**:

| Reward | |
| :--- | :--- |
| 🪙 **+10c** | A bonus from the handler |
| 🤝 **+1 Trust** | The handler likes crews who don't make noise |
| 📈 **+1 Mark** | See [Progression](progression.md#-marks) |

> 🎯 **Clean jobs are the best way to play.** They pay more, earn Trust faster and level the crew faster. Going in loud is always possible, but it costs.

## 📉 Reducing Exposure

| Method | Effect | When |
| :--- | :---: | :--- |
| 🌙 **Things go quiet** | `-1` | Automatically, every downtime |
| 🤫 **Keep Quiet** (downtime action) | `-1` (`-2` with [Safehouse I](community.md#-projects)) | Once per downtime, however many characters take it |
| 🫥 **Lie low** (skip a job) | `-2` (`-3` with Safehouse II) | Instead of taking a job. No pay, but the Need is still due. Things still go quiet afterwards |
| 🧹 **Cleaner** in the Sunk | `-1` | 20c, once per downtime |
| 🎁 **Clean-up** [favour](#-favours) | `-1` | Spend one handler favour |
| ✨ **Forget-me draught** ([Refined](equipment.md#-consumables)) | `-1` | A witness drinks it before the end of the job |
| 🖋️ **Doctored Records** ([Forger talent](progression.md#-trait-talents)) | `-1` | Once per downtime. Doesn't stack with a Cleaner |
| 🏚️ **Safehouse III** | Set 10 to 8 | Once per campaign |

## 🚦 Exposure Bands

| Exposure | Band | Effect |
| :---: | :--- | :--- |
| **0–3** | 🟢 **Quiet** | No effect |
| **4–6** | 🟡 **Noticed** | Rivals know who the crew works for. Make a [Raid check](community.md#️-raids) at every Homecoming |
| **7–9** | 🟠 **Hot** | As Noticed, and: Raid checks at `+1 dice`, Raid tests at `-1 dice`. Job pay **-10c**. The handler offers **one fewer favour** |
| **10** | 🔴 **Burned** | See below |

### 🔴 Burned

At Exposure 10, **Antioch cuts the crew loose**.

- **No more jobs** from the handler, and **no favours**.
- **No protection.** Nobody comes when the district is raided: a failed Raid test costs **2 Community**.
- **The water may be cut** ([Community: the Water](community.md#-the-water)). The DM decides.
- **Trust -2.**

**Coming back:**

| Trust | What happens |
| :---: | :--- |
| **5+** | The handler argues for the crew and offers a **penance job**: Dangerous, and **unpaid**. If it succeeds, Exposure drops to **6**, the water comes back, and jobs start again. If it fails, the crew stays Burned |
| **0–4** | The handler won't risk their name. The crew is on its own. A rival house may make an offer. This is a turning point for the campaign, not an ending |

## 🤝 Trust

**Trust** is a number from **0 to 10** on the crew sheet. It's the handler's opinion of the crew. A new campaign starts at **3**.

### Gaining and losing Trust

| Event | Trust |
| :--- | :---: |
| ✅ The job's objective was completed | `+1` |
| ✨ A [clean job](#-clean-jobs) | `+1` |
| 🗞️ Bringing the handler something useful they didn't ask for | `+1` |
| ❌ The objective failed | `-1` |
| 🧥 A loaned item lost or damaged | `-1` |
| 🙅 Disobeying the handler's instructions, and it caused trouble | `-1` |
| 🔴 Becoming **Burned** | `-2` |
| 🤥 Caught lying to the handler | `-2` |
| 🏛️ Caught working for another house | `-3` |

### Trust bands

| Trust | Band | Favours per job | The handler… |
| :---: | :--- | :---: | :--- |
| **0–2** | 🧊 **Cold** | 0 | Gives only what the crew needs to know. **The DM leaves one important detail out of every briefing** |
| **3–5** | 🤝 **Working** | 1 | Does the job, and nothing more |
| **6–8** | 🫱 **Trusted** | 2 | Will lend **Refined** gear |
| **9–10** | 🗝️ **Confidant** | 3 | **Once per campaign**, takes the blame for a disaster: **-3 Exposure** |

**Hot** crews (Exposure 7–9) get **one fewer** favour.

## 🎁 Favours

At the job briefing, the crew asks for favours, up to the number their Trust allows. Unused favours don't carry over.

| Favour | Effect |
| :--- | :--- |
| 🧥 **Loan** | Borrow one item for the job, from any market the crew can reach. **Refined** items need Trust 6+. Return it afterwards, or **-1 Trust** |
| 📜 **Papers** | A forged invitation, a uniform or a disguise. Counts as a **tool** (`+1 dice`) on tests to pass as someone who belongs |
| 🚪 **Livery escort** | The handler walks the crew through **one** upper gate or checkpoint. No test |
| 🗞️ **Intel** | Ask the DM **one question** about the job. The answer is true |
| 🧹 **Clean-up** | **-1 Exposure** after the job |
| 🚨 **Extraction** *(costs 2 favours)* | Once during the job, the crew gets out of one bad situation: an arrest, a locked building, a dead end |

## 🔒 GM Only: the Handler's Secret

> ⚠️ **Don't share this section with players.** The full secret is in the World Bible (*GM secret: House Calyx and the Orrery*).

The handler is secretly an agent of the **Orrery**. Trust measures the handler's opinion of the crew, but also **how far the handler is willing to involve them**.

- **Trust 6+.** The handler starts making small mistakes on purpose. A dried flower left on a table. A name that doesn't belong to Antioch.
- **Trust 9+.** The handler may ask for **one job that isn't for Antioch**: no pay, no favours, just *"I need this done, and I need you not to ask."* Accepting gives `+1 Trust`. It's the first real hint of the Orrery.
- **If the Orrery's orders change** (for example, the Dawnward takes over and Antioch must fall), Trust **stays the same**. Favours may quietly lead the crew into jobs that weaken Antioch. That's the planned twist: the person the crew trusts most is working against the thing protecting their home.

## 📖 Worked Example

<details open>
<summary><b>A loud job</b></summary>

> **Start:** Exposure **2** (Quiet), Trust **5** (Working, 1 favour).
>
> **Briefing.** The crew uses its one favour on **Papers**: Terraces servants' uniforms.
>
> **The job.** A guard sees Mara's face (`+1`). The alarm goes up (`+1`). Jonah drops a guard with his rifle, which is Conspicuous in the Terraces (`+1`), and leaves the body (`+1`). They get the ledger.
>
> Exposure **6** (Noticed). That's 4 gained, so it isn't a clean job.
>
> **Trust:** objective completed `+1` → **6** (Trusted). Next job, they'll get 2 favours.
>
> **Homecoming.** Exposure is 4+, so they make a **Raid check**. 🎲 9: **there's a raid.**
>
> **Downtime.** Things go quiet (`-1`) and Dace takes Keep Quiet (`-1`). Exposure **4**. Still Noticed. Next time, maybe a quieter approach.

</details>

## ✅ Design Decisions

| # | Question | Ruling |
| :---: | :--- | :--- |
| **1** | What is Exposure? | 🟡 **Proposed:** a crew-wide 0–10 track that everyone can see. It rises during jobs and falls in downtime. |
| **2** | What happens at 10? | 🟡 **Proposed:** Burned. No jobs, no protection, and the water may be cut. Coming back needs Trust 5+ and an unpaid penance job. |
| **3** | What is Trust? | 🟡 **Proposed:** a 0–10 track, starting at 3, that decides how many favours the handler gives each job. |
| **4** | Do the players see the tracks? | 🟡 **Proposed:** yes, both. The handler's **secret** stays hidden, not the number. |

🟡 = proposed, not yet final. The handler's secret comes from *Project Society — World Bible* (GM secret: House Calyx and the Orrery).
