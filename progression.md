# 📈 Project Society — Progression

> **The crew rises together.** Every job earns Marks. Every 4 Marks is a new level.
> Levels bring new traits, talents and contacts, more wounds, and access to better markets.

## 🔖 Marks

At the end of every job, **every character on the job** earns the same Marks:

| Job | Marks |
| :--- | :---: |
| 🟢 Simple | 1 |
| 🟡 Standard | 2 |
| 🔴 Dangerous | 3 |
| ✨ [Clean job](exposure_and_trust.md#-clean-jobs) (0–1 Exposure gained) | `+1` |
| ❌ Objective failed | **1 Mark** in total, whatever the job |

**Every 4 Marks is a new level.** Level up during [downtime](actions.md#-downtime).

Because everyone earns the same Marks, **the crew levels together**. The **crew level** is the level most of the crew is at.

> 🧮 **Pacing.** Level 10 takes **36 Marks**: about 18 Standard jobs, or 12 clean ones. That's roughly the same pace as building Community from 3 to 10.

> 🛒 **Markets** are explained in [Equipment](equipment.md#-markets). A [Workshop III](community.md#-projects) or a [Sunk Dealer](#-contacts) can open them earlier.

## ⭐ Advances

Each level from 2 to 10 gives **one advance**. Choose one:

| Advance | What you get | Limit |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 🧬 **New trait** | Any trait from the [trait list](character_creation.md#-trait-list). Describe how you learned it | **6 traits** at most |
| 🌟 **Talent** | The [talent](#-talents) for a trait you already have | One per trait |
| 🤝 **Contact** | A person from the [contact list](#-contacts) | **3 contacts** at most |

> 🧭 **Traits vs talents.** A new trait makes you *broader*: more rolls get `+1 dice`. A talent makes you *better at what you already do*: it adds a special rule. Both are good choices.

## 🌟 Talents

Each trait has one talent. You must have the trait to take it.

| Trait | Talent | Effect |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 👊 Brawler | **Haymaker** | When your melee wound roll gives **Shaken**, the target gains **2 Shaken** instead |
| 🎯 Marksman | **Dead Eye** | **Aiming** gives `+2 dice` instead of `+1` |
| 🗝️ Thief | **Quick Hands** | In combat, picking a lock or using an item takes your **Move** instead of your Action |
| 🔨 Craftsperson | **Jury-rig** | Once per job, improvise any **Works tool** from what's lying around. It lasts for one scene |
| ⚙️ Mechanic | **Overpressure** | Once per combat, `+1 dice` To Wound with a firearm or boiler lance. Afterwards it hisses and smokes, and the DM may call it Conspicuous for the rest of the job |
| 🩹 Medic | **Field Surgeon** | **Patch Up** can remove **1 wound** instead of Shaken |
| 🗣️ Smooth Talker | **Silver Tongue** | Once per job, turn a failed social test into a pass. The DM names a price you must accept: a promise, a debt or a secret |
| 😠 Intimidator | **Menace** | A new combat Action: choose an enemy within 6" who can see you and make an Intimidator test. On a pass the enemy gains **1 Shaken** (on a critical, **2**) |
| 🏚️ Streetwise | **Ear to the Ground** | One free **Informant** question each job |
| 🎩 Courtier | **Belongs Here** | In the Terraces and Heights, the first time each job someone questions why you're there, they accept your answer. No test |
| 🏃 Runner | **Fleet-footed** | Move **8"** instead of 6" |
| 🌑 Shadow | **Ghost** | When you Attack from **Hide**, `+1 dice` To Hit and To Wound. Afterwards you're no longer hidden |
| 🚂 Driver | **Getaway** | Once per job, if you're driving, the crew escapes a scene. No test |
| 🖋️ Forger | **Paper Trail** | Once per job, produce a document you "made earlier". It counts as a **Papers** [favour](exposure_and_trust.md#-favours) |
| 📚 Lettered | **Research** | Once per job, ask the DM one question about a person, place or house. The answer is true |

> ⚖️ **Talents don't break the dice system.** None of them changes a gear rating or the `-3` cap. They add dice in narrow situations, or let you skip a roll once per job.

## 🤝 Contacts

A contact is a person, with a name the player chooses. Contacts can be hurt, bought or lost, and the DM can use them in the story, including [Hardship events](community.md#-hardship-events).

| Contact | Who they are | Effect |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 🏷️ **Fence** | Moves goods with no questions | Sell gear for **¾** price. Doesn't stack with the Workshop |
| ✨ **Sunk Dealer** | Trades stolen Refinement | The **Refined market** opens, whatever your level |
| 🩺 **Clinic Doctor** | Owes you their life, or their licence | One free **street doctor** visit (remove 2 wounds) every downtime |
| 🗞️ **Informant** | Hears everything in three districts | One free **Informant** question each job |
| 🎩 **Antioch Footman** | A servant inside the Antioch household | Once per job, a **Livery escort** without spending a favour |
| 🧓 **Gang Elder** | Respected in the district | `+1 dice` on **Hardship** tests |

## 🆕 New and Replacement Characters

When a character dies or retires, or a new player joins:

- They start at **crew level minus one**. With [Hall II](community.md#-projects), they start at the **full** crew level.
- They get every reward for the levels below that: an advance for each level from 2 up, Hardened at 4 and 8, and market access.
- They have **Marks** equal to the minimum for their level.
- They start with their background kit and **20 chips**, like everyone else. They don't inherit the dead character's gear unless the crew gives it to them.

## ✅ Design Decisions

| # | Question | Ruling |
| :---: | :--- | :--- |
| **1** | How do characters advance? | ✅ **Level progression** that unlocks traits, talents, contacts, gear and wounds. |
| **2** | How are levels earned? | 🟡 **Proposed:** Marks from jobs (1 / 2 / 3, +1 for a clean job), 4 Marks per level, 10 levels. |
| **3** | Does each player level separately? | 🟡 **Proposed:** no. Everyone on a job earns the same Marks, so the crew levels together. |
| **4** | What do levels unlock? | 🟡 **Proposed:** an advance every level, Hardened at 4 and 8, the Specialist market at 3 and the Refined market at 6. |
| **5** | Limits | 🟡 **Proposed:** 6 traits, one talent per trait, 3 contacts, 8 wounds. |

🟡 = proposed, not yet final.
