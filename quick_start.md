## 🎭 What You Play

You're a **crew** from a poor district in the Works. Your gang works for **Antioch**, a noble house that keeps the district's water running. In return, the crew does Antioch's dirty work: break-ins, sabotage, kidnappings.

Every job pays **chips**. The crew decides how much to keep and how much to send home to the district. Don't get traced back to Antioch.

More: [Getting Started](getting_started.md).

## 🪪 Your Character

| You have | What it means |
| :--- | :--- |
| **3 traits** | Things you're good at. Two from your background, one of your choice. A trait that covers a roll gives `+1 dice` |
| **5 wounds** | Fill the last box and you're **Dying** |
| **Gear** | Your background's kit, plus **20 chips** to spend. Weapons help you wound, armour stops you being wounded, tools help with tests |
| **Ties** | Someone at home who depends on you, and why you still work for Antioch |
| **Level 1** | Jobs earn **Marks**. Every 4 Marks is a level |

There's no skill list. When you try something risky, say which trait covers it and why.

More: [Character Creation](character_creation.md).

## 🎲 Rolling Dice

**Roll 2D6. 7 or more passes.**

- **Bonuses** (`+dice`): a covering trait, a tool, a helper, an easy situation, aiming.
- **Penalties** (`-dice`): a hard situation, no trait when it needs know-how, cover, long range, being Shaken.
- **Cancel** bonuses against penalties. For each point left over, roll **one extra die**.
- **Keep two dice.** Net bonus: keep the two **highest**. Net penalty: keep the two **lowest**. Penalties never go worse than `-3`.
- **Double 6** is a **critical**. **Double 1** is a **fumble**: in a fight, your turn ends.

Only the crew rolls tests. If a guard is alert, your test is Hard. If they're drunk, it's Easy.

More: [Dice Modifiers](dice_modifiers.md) lists every bonus and penalty.

## ⚔️ Fighting

The crew and the enemies take turns activating **one model each**.

On your activation you get **one Move** (up to 6") and **one Action**: Attack, Aim (uses your Move), Dash, Patch Up, Stabilise, Use Item, Hide, Help, Interact or Stunt.

An attack is **two rolls**:

1. **To Hit**: 7+ hits. **Brawler** (melee) or **Marksman** (ranged) gives `+1`. Aiming gives `+1`.
2. **To Wound**: your **weapon** adds dice and their **armour** takes them away. Read the total:

| To Wound total | Result |
| :---: | :--- |
| **2–6** | **Shaken**: `-1 dice` on everything until the fight ends |
| **7–11** | **1 wound** |
| **12** | **3 wounds**, and you get a free attack |

More: [Actions](actions.md#️-combat-turns).

## 🩸 Getting Hurt

- **Shaken** stacks to `-3`. After that, each Shaken result is a wound instead. All Shaken clears when the fight ends.
- **Dying**: your last wound box is filled. You can't act. After the fight, a crewmate makes a **survival roll** (a Medic test). Pass and you live with a **Scar**. Fail and you die.
- **Healing**: Patch Up removes Shaken in a fight. Wounds come back in downtime, or with the right consumables.

More: [Healing](actions.md#-healing) and [Dying](actions.md#-dying).

## 🪙 After the Job

1. **Homecoming.** The pay comes home. The district needs some of it (the **Need**). The rest goes into the **Pot** to build projects, or gets split between the crew.
2. **Marks.** Everyone on the job earns the same Marks. Every 4 Marks is a level, and each level from 2 gives you an **advance**: a new trait, a talent or a contact.
3. **Downtime.** Recover, take one downtime action, level up and shop.

The crew also tracks **Exposure** (how close you are to being traced) and **Trust** (how much the handler will do for you).

More: [Community](community.md), [Exposure & Trust](exposure_and_trust.md) and [Progression](progression.md).

## 🆕 Your First Session

1. **Make a character.** About ten minutes. Make sure the crew can cover fighting, sneaking, talking and patching people up.
2. **Name the district** and meet the handler.
3. **Play a first job.** One fight at most, so everyone learns the rules.
4. **Play Homecoming in full.** That's where the game's choices are.

Keep this page and [Dice Modifiers](dice_modifiers.md) open at the table. Tap any underlined term in the app to see its rule.
