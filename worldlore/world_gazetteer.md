## ✨ Places in the Heights

### 🌿 The Conservatory
- **Where:** On the Crust just off the northern Brink, under the highest point of the Veil.
- **What it is:** The Crown's glasshouses of living glass, each bigger than a Works district, built round a small vent of their own. The Keepers grow all the Refinement on Allaneus here from guarded seed-stock. Warm, green and silent inside, and smelling of wet earth and breathmoss.
- **Why a crew might go there:** You almost certainly won't. Harming a Keeper is one of the few things every house agrees is unforgivable. But everything Refined starts here, and a house that wanted something grown in secret would need someone inside.

### 🪙 The Crown Mint
- **Where:** Beside the Conservatory, joined to it by a covered bridge.
- **What it is:** Where chips are grown, counted and sent out to the Seats. Run by Keepers, guarded by Greycoats.
- **Why a crew might go there:** The **Nine of Brasswater** robbed it once. Nobody's done it since. People say there's still a reward for them.

### 🏛️ The Seat Hall
- **Where:** The southern Brink, between Corvane's Register and the Lockgates.
- **What it is:** A long hall of white grown stone where the Monarch's writs are read and the Seats are granted. Every great house has a chair here, and the empty chairs of Mereth and Hollin are never taken away.
- **Why a crew might go there:** The Assessor will sit here. Every house wants to know what's said in this room, and the Seat Hall's records go back to the Charter.

### 💧 Tidehouse
- **Where:** The eastern Brink, beside the melt-houses, where the Haul Road ends.
- **What it is:** Antioch's house. Grown blue glass that looks like ice, and is always warm. The family's first crawler, *Patience*, stands in its great hall.
- **Why a crew might go there:** Your handler might. You won't, unless something has gone very wrong, or very right.

### 🔥 The Ember Court
- **Where:** The western Brink, facing Tidehouse across the Well, with Cinderwell's smoke behind it.
- **What it is:** Varre's house, of black glass that glows faintly orange at night. Varre's foundry contracts, Antioch's embarrassments and thirty years of grudges are kept here.
- **Why a crew might go there:** Because Antioch wants to know what Varre knows.

### 🥀 The Hanging Gardens
- **Where:** The north-eastern Brink, spilling over the edge of the Well in terraces of green.
- **What it is:** House Calyx's gardens, the most beautiful place in the city. Every tree in them grows over one of the Heights' dead. The perfumeries are here, and the pressing-rooms where Calyx makes its dried flowers.
- **Why a crew might go there:** Nobles bury their secrets with their dead. And the gardens are open to mourners, if you have a black coat and a sad face.

### ⚓ The Skyquay
- **Where:** High on the southern Rimwall, forty kilometres from the Well, far past the Veil and open to the cold. An hour from the Brink by the Quay Line.
- **What it is:** Antioch's fleet yards, where ships come down from the sky on columns of steam and noise, and the ore barges come down from the Ember. Here too the Netwrights bring the catch of the Nets, and auction it off. Cranes, dry docks, warehouses, and the **Customs House**, where every cargo leaving Allaneus is counted and taxed.
- **Why a crew might go there:** Cargo goes missing. Cargo gets swapped. A ship in dock is a house on the ground with a lot of doors. And the yard crews are Works folk who might let you in.

### 🏚️ The Mereth Estate
- **Where:** The northern Brink, between the Lantern and the Gardens.
- **What it is:** House Mereth's mansion, shut since 366 AC. Its Refined doors still know Mereth's family, and nobody else. The Terraces say it's haunted. The Gazette says it's for sale, and has said so for forty-six years.
- **Why a crew might go there:** Whatever Mereth was hiding when it fell might still be inside.

## 🌲 Places on the Crust

The Heights doesn't stop at the Brink. The whole floor of the Rim is theirs.

### 🌾 The Sowings
- **Where:** The warm Crust between the Well and the Cradle, south and south-east.
- **What it is:** Aurel's grain fields, field after field of wheat from other worlds growing in soil the vents keep warm. Steam drifts across them from the Smokes. Twice a year the Works field gangs come up to bring in the harvest.
- **Why a crew might go there:** A field-gang day pass is the easiest way for a Works face to get onto the Crust legally. Grain silos burn beautifully. And the Newmouth is out there.

### 🆕 The Newmouth
- **Where:** In the middle of the Sowings.
- **What it is:** The new vent opened by the Great Shudder of 405 AC, about a hundred metres across and still growing. The Keepers ringed it with a fence of living glass within a day. Greycoats guard the fence. Keepers come and go at night.
- **Why a crew might go there:** Every house wants to know what the Keepers are doing in it. Aurel wants it most.

### 🌲 The Blackwood
- **Where:** All round the Maw, north of the Well.
- **What it is:** The biggest ventwood forest on the Crust, thick and black and warm, fed by the Maw's breath. The houses hunt in it. Calyx's foresters keep it. The trees near the Maw's edge glow faintly at night.
- **Why a crew might go there:** Hunting parties are where nobles talk most freely and are least guarded. And things get buried in the Blackwood that aren't meant to be found.

### 🌲 The Hollow
- **Where:** North-west of the Well.
- **What it is:** House Hollin's well, which went cold in the 240s AC. A crusted plug of grey rock, frosted at the edges, with a ventwood forest growing on it that nobody planted. Hollin's estate was here. You can still find its foundation stones under the moss.
- **Why a crew might go there:** Nobody builds here, nobody guards it, and nobody upstairs will talk about it. That's three reasons.

### 🚂 The Haul Road
- **Where:** From the Icegate in the eastern Rimwall to Tidehouse on the Brink.
- **What it is:** Forty kilometres of crushed-stone road across the Crust, built for ice crawlers. It runs between noble estates, and the crawlers rattle past the garden walls day and night. Every Antioch crawler is guarded by the Keel on its way in.
- **Why a crew might go there:** A crawler carries a month's water for a hundred districts. Somebody would pay a great deal to see one stopped.

### 🫨 The Listening Posts
- **Where:** All over the Crust, a few kilometres apart.
- **What they are:** Small domes of living glass, waist high, that hum when the ground moves. They're the Keepers'. Nobody else knows how to read them.
- **Why a crew might go there:** A house that could read them would know when the next big shudder is coming. That's worth more than a Seat.

## 🎩 Places in the Terraces

### 🚧 The Lockgates
- **Where:** Where the Terraces meet the Brink, at the top of every main stair and Hoist.
- **What it is:** Greycoat checkpoints of iron and grown glass. Papers, Livery, or a very good bribe.
- **Why a crew might go there:** Every job in the Heights starts by getting through one.

### ⛓️ The Drop
- **Where:** Hanging over the open Well, below the southern Terraces.
- **What it is:** Corvane's prison: iron cages on long chains, swinging over the Murk. Prisoners can see the whole city and can't reach any of it. The cages are raised and lowered by a steam winch, and the winch house is the only way in or out.
- **Why a crew might go there:** To get someone out. Or to make sure someone doesn't get out, before they talk.

### 📰 The Gazette Press
- **Where:** The northern Terraces, below the Lantern.
- **What it is:** Lysander's printing hall. The presses run all night and the Gazette is on every Terraces doorstep by first bell.
- **Why a crew might go there:** To stop a story before it prints. Or to make sure one does.

### 🎓 The Stewards' Exchange
- **Where:** The middle Terraces.
- **What it is:** A coffee house and hiring hall where the houses' senior servants meet, gossip, and hire staff. References are checked here. Careers are ended here.
- **Why a crew might go there:** Want to get someone into a house as a servant? Want to know which footman drinks? This is where you ask.

### 🫖 The Saltmarsh Rooms
- **Where:** The lower Terraces, near the Span Hoist.
- **What it is:** A respectable boarding house for Terraces clerks, run by a widow who doesn't ask questions as long as the rent's on time. Half the Fixers in the Margin have rented a room here at some point.
- **Why a crew might go there:** A safe bed above the Span, if you can pass for respectable.

## 🤝 Places in the Margin

### 🍺 The Halfway House
- **Where:** Where the north bridges of the Span meet.
- **What it is:** The Margin's oldest tavern. Every fixer drinks here. The back room is where **the Brokers' Table** sits, and where the Margin Peace is kept.
- **Why a crew might go there:** To find a fixer, a buyer, a forger, or a job. To be seen to be respectable, by Margin standards.

### 🏛️ The Glass Market
- **Where:** The east side of the Span.
- **What it is:** The biggest market hall in the city, under a roof of cracked glass. Terraces goods, Works goods, off-world goods. Mostly legal.
- **Why a crew might go there:** Shopping, meetings, and the kind of crowd it's easy to disappear into.

### 🪝 Hook Street
- **Where:** The lowest gallery of the Span, wet with the Weep.
- **What it is:** Cheap bars, cheap rooms, Specialist dealers, gang armourers, and doctors who don't write anything down.
- **Why a crew might go there:** It's where the Specialist market lives.

### 🌉 The Long Bridge
- **Where:** Right across the Well, east to west.
- **What it is:** The only bridge that crosses the whole Well, twelve kilometres of iron and cable over nothing, held up by the tallest stacks on the floor. Duels are fought on it at dawn. It's the only place on the Span where the Margin Peace doesn't hold, by long tradition, as long as both sides agreed to meet.
- **Why a crew might go there:** To settle something. Or to watch someone else settle something.

### 🔀 The Crossings
- **Where:** From the Span to every other living well of the Rim.
- **What it is:** The Margin's tunnels: wide, lamplit bores lined with stalls, inns, tollhouses and fixers' offices. The Cinderwell Crossing smells of hot metal. The Cradle Crossing smells of bread. The Margin Peace holds all the way along.
- **Why a crew might go there:** The safest way into another well. And everybody you need to bribe has an office in one.

## 🔩 Places in the Works

### 💧 The Great Pumps
- **Where:** The eastern floor, at the bottom of Antioch's main pressure lines.
- **What it is:** Antioch's pumping station: a cathedral of iron, steam and noise, where the water from the melt-houses on the Brink is driven out to more than a hundred districts. The **Gauger-General** runs it, and every district Gauger answers to her.
- **Why a crew might go there:** Because it's the heart of everything Antioch holds over your district. And because every other house would like to see it fail, just once, just before the Assessor arrives.

### 🔥 The Kindling
- **Where:** The centre of the Well's floor, round the edge of the Throat.
- **What it is:** The great deep boilers that drink the heat rising out of the Throat, the open hole in the middle of the floor where the vent still breathes, and drive everything in the city. So hot that the Weep turns back to steam before it lands. The stokers' shrines line every boiler. Breathmoss grows thick round the Throat's lip, and the stokers won't let anyone cut it.
- **Why a crew might go there:** Sabotage. Or to hide something where nobody will look, because nobody can stand the heat long enough.

### 🥊 The Kettle
- **Where:** The northern floor, between three districts that all claim it.
- **What it is:** An old boiler hall turned fighting pit. Every Slack Day, champions from every district fight for chips, pride and their gang's name. The betting is run by the Sunk.
- **Why a crew might go there:** Kettle money. Meetings with other gangs on neutral ground. And everyone who matters in the Works comes to the big fights.

### 🪜 The Thousand Steps
- **Where:** Up the northern wall, from the floor to the Span.
- **What it is:** The great public stair, iron and stone, zigzagging up the wall with landings, shrines, stalls and beggars. It's a lot more than a thousand steps. Nobody's counted since the Great Shudder took a flight out, and the gap is still bridged with planks.
- **Why a crew might go there:** It's the only way up that's free.

### 🏭 Slagside
- **Where:** The western floor.
- **What it is:** Varre's biggest foundry district, and home of the **Slagborn**. The slag heaps glow at night. The water is the worst in the Works.
- **Why a crew might go there:** Because Varre's crew lives here. Because the Slagborn want better water and your district has it.

### 🪦 Low Mereth
- **Where:** The southern floor, against the wall.
- **What it is:** The only district that never signed with another house after Mereth fell. It's had no house water for forty-six years, and it's still there. Nobody knows how. The people of Low Mereth don't say.
- **Why a crew might go there:** If there's a way to live without a house's water, it's here.

### 🚂 Railhead
- **Where:** Halfway along the Run from the Well to Cinderwell.
- **What it is:** A shanty district built along the sides of the Run, where the trains stop to take on water. Home of **the Railers**, who take a toll on every train and sell passage to anyone who'd rather not be seen.
- **Why a crew might go there:** It's the back way into Varre's well.

## 🛶 Places in the Sunk

### 🛶 Gutterlight
- **Where:** The biggest flooded hall in the upper Sunk.
- **What it is:** A floating market of barges lashed together, lit by lamps hung from the ceiling on long chains. Stolen goods, forged papers, and the city's only market in stolen Refinement.
- **Why a crew might go there:** The Refined market. Fences. Anything you can't buy anywhere else.

### ⛪ The Drowned Chapel
- **Where:** Down a flooded stair from Gutterlight.
- **What it is:** A Bonded hall, older than anything else in the city, half underwater. Carvings on the walls show people, boilers, and something round and glowing at the bottom of the Well. The lenders meet here. Nobody draws a blade inside.
- **Why a crew might go there:** To borrow chips. To pay them back. To beg for more time.

### 🚪 Mereth's Gate
- **Where:** The deep galleries, below the lamps.
- **What it is:** A great iron door in the rock, stamped with Mereth's crown, and welded shut from the outside. Nobody knows what's behind it. The ferrymen won't stop near it.
- **Why a crew might go there:** Somebody's always offering to pay a crew to open it.

### 🌀 The Sump
- **Where:** The lowest open water in the Sunk.
- **What it is:** A black lake in a cavern nobody's mapped, where the old galleries open into the throat of the vent. The bitterwater glows faintly here, and it's warm enough to steam. The ferrymen go no further.
- **Why a crew might go there:** Don't.

### 🧱 The Hollin Wall
- **Where:** At the end of the old bore that once ran to the Hollow.
- **What it is:** A wall of brick and iron across the bore, laid in the 240s AC, stamped with every Charter House's mark except Hollin's. Water seeps through it. Sometimes, ferrymen say, so does light.
- **Why a crew might go there:** To find out what's on the other side.

## 🕳️ The Other Wells

The Well is the biggest living well in the Rim, but not the only one. Each of these has its own Terraces, Works and Sunk, joined to the Well by bores. See [The Rim and the Sky](world_rim.md#-the-wells-of-the-rim).

### ⚫ Cinderwell
- **Where:** West of the Well, joined to it by the Slag Bore and the Cinderwell Run.
- **What it is:** Varre's well, six kilometres across, its walls black with coal and streaked red with iron. Foundry districts all the way down, and the ore from the Ember is smelted here. The air is thick and the Weep comes down grey.
- **Why a crew might go there:** Because Varre keeps its secrets at home, and home is here.

### 🌾 The Cradle
- **Where:** South of the Well, past the Sowings.
- **What it is:** Aurel's well: shallow, wide and gentle, its walls terraced with vat-farms and long glass roofs. The warmest, cleanest Works in the Rim. Tallow Steps and the Sheaves are here.
- **Why a crew might go there:** Aurel's ration ledgers are kept in the Cradle's Terraces. Change one line and a district eats, or doesn't.

### 🔴 The Maw
- **Where:** North of the Well, in the middle of the Blackwood.
- **What it is:** The biggest vent in the Rim, nearly twenty kilometres across, too hot to live in, with no bottom anyone has found. It glows red at night and breathes out more air than every other vent together. The Works calls it the Mother's Mouth.
- **Why a crew might go there:** To throw something in that must never be found. People have.

## 🧊 Places Outside

### 🧊 The Icegate
- **Where:** A pass through the eastern Rimwall.
- **What it is:** Antioch's gate between the Rim and the Pale: a fortified cutting through the mountains, with crawler sheds, fuel tanks, a hauler barracks and a Keel garrison. On one side, warm air. On the other, the wind.
- **Why a crew might go there:** Everything that leaves the Rim by land goes through here. So does everything that comes back.

### 🧊 The Ice Roads
- **Where:** Out across the Pale, from the Icegate to the glaciers.
- **What it is:** Antioch's crawler routes: hundreds of kilometres of track across frozen stone, marked with iron posts and shelter huts, running from Green to Green along the Seams. The **Shelter at Nine Mile**, on the last wayvent before the glaciers, is the last place a hauler can turn back.
- **Why a crew might go there:** Somebody wants an ice crawler to go missing. Somebody else wants it to arrive.

### 🏔️ The Glaciers
- **Where:** Weeks out on the Pale.
- **What it is:** Where the ice comes from. Antioch's cutting camps work the edges. Haulers say there are old things frozen in the ice: machines, ships, and once, something that looked like a door.
- **Why a crew might go there:** Almost nobody does. But almost nobody isn't nobody.

### 🟢 The Greens
- **Where:** Strung along the Seams across the Pale.
- **What they are:** Small wild vents, each a patch of breathmoss, ventwood and steam in the ice. The ones on the ice roads are **wayvents**, with hauler huts. The others are wild, or belong to **the Unsworn**, families who left the Society and the houses say don't exist.
- **Why a crew might go there:** Someone who needs to vanish completely could live on a Green. If the Unsworn let them.

## 🌙 Places in the Sky

Nobody from the Works goes here on purpose. See [The Three Moons](world_rim.md#-the-three-moons).

| Place | What it is |
| :--- | :--- |
| ⚪ **The Salt** | The great white moon. The Crown's mines, where the metal for chips comes from. Contract miners sign for ten years, and come home rich or not at all |
| 🔴 **The Ember** | The red moon. Varre's mines, under the Ember Lease. The ore comes down on Antioch's barges to the Skyquay |
| ⚪ **The Shard** | The small fast moon. The Netwrights' station, and Corvane's penal mine, the **Shardworks** |
| ✨ **The Nets** | Great nets in orbit that catch everything left in the sky. When one crosses in sunlight, that's the Sweep |
