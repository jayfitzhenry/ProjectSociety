## ✨ Places in the Heights

### 🌿 The Conservatory
- **Where:** The centre of the rim, under the highest point of the Veil.
- **What it is:** The Crown's dome of living glass, where the Keepers grow all the Refinement on Allaneus from guarded seed-stock. Warm, green and silent inside, and smelling of wet earth.
- **Why a crew might go there:** You almost certainly won't. Harming a Keeper is one of the few things every house agrees is unforgivable. But everything Refined starts here, and a house that wanted something grown in secret would need someone inside.

### 🪙 The Crown Mint
- **Where:** Beside the Conservatory, joined to it by a covered bridge.
- **What it is:** Where chips are grown, counted and sent out to the Seats. Run by Keepers, guarded by Greycoats.
- **Why a crew might go there:** The **Nine of Brasswater** robbed it once. Nobody's done it since. People say there's still a reward for them.

### 🏛️ The Seat Hall
- **Where:** The southern rim, between Corvane's Register and the Lockgates.
- **What it is:** A long hall of white grown stone where the Monarch's writs are read and the Seats are granted. Every great house has a chair here, and the empty chairs of Mereth and Hollin are never taken away.
- **Why a crew might go there:** The Assessor will sit here. Every house wants to know what's said in this room, and the Seat Hall's records go back to the Charter.

### 💧 Tidehouse
- **Where:** The eastern rim, beside the melt-houses.
- **What it is:** Antioch's house. Grown blue glass that looks like ice, and is always warm. The family's first crawler, *Patience*, stands in its great hall.
- **Why a crew might go there:** Your handler might. You won't, unless something has gone very wrong, or very right.

### 🔥 The Ember Court
- **Where:** The western rim, facing Tidehouse across the Well.
- **What it is:** Varre's house, of black glass that glows faintly orange at night. Varre's foundry contracts, Antioch's embarrassments and thirty years of grudges are kept here.
- **Why a crew might go there:** Because Antioch wants to know what Varre knows.

### 🥀 The Hanging Gardens
- **Where:** The north-eastern rim, spilling over the edge of the Well in terraces of green.
- **What it is:** House Calyx's gardens, the most beautiful place in the city. Every tree in them grows over one of the Heights' dead. The perfumeries are here, and the pressing-rooms where Calyx makes its dried flowers.
- **Why a crew might go there:** Nobles bury their secrets with their dead. And the gardens are open to mourners, if you have a black coat and a sad face.

### ⚓ The Skyquay
- **Where:** The southern rim, past the edge of the Veil, open to the cold.
- **What it is:** Antioch's fleet yards, where ships come down from the sky on columns of steam and noise. Cranes, dry docks, warehouses, and the **Customs House**, where every cargo leaving Allaneus is counted and taxed.
- **Why a crew might go there:** Cargo goes missing. Cargo gets swapped. A ship in dock is a house on the ground with a lot of doors. And the yard crews are Works folk who might let you in.

### 🏚️ The Mereth Estate
- **Where:** The northern rim, between the Lantern and the Gardens.
- **What it is:** House Mereth's mansion, shut since 366 AC. Its Refined doors still know Mereth's family, and nobody else. The Terraces say it's haunted. The Gazette says it's for sale, and has said so for forty-six years.
- **Why a crew might go there:** Whatever Mereth was hiding when it fell might still be inside.

## 🎩 Places in the Terraces

### 🚧 The Lockgates
- **Where:** Where the Terraces meet the rim, at the top of every main stair and Hoist.
- **What it is:** Greycoat checkpoints of iron and grown glass. Papers, Livery, or a very good bribe.
- **Why a crew might go there:** Every job in the Heights starts by getting through one.

### ⛓️ The Drop
- **Where:** Hanging over the open Well, below the southern Terraces.
- **What it is:** Corvane's prison: iron cages on long chains, swinging over the Murk. Prisoners can see the whole city and can't reach any of it. The cages are raised and lowered by a steam winch, and the winch house is the only way in or out.
- **Why a crew might go there:** To get someone out. Or to make sure someone doesn't get out, before they talk.

### 📰 The Gazette Press
- **Where:** The northern Terraces, below the Lantern.
- **What it is:** Lysander's printing hall. The presses run all night and the Gazette is on every Terraces doorstep by first bell.
- **Why a crew might go there:** To stop a story before it prints. Or to make sure one does.

### 🎓 The Stewards' Exchange
- **Where:** The middle Terraces.
- **What it is:** A coffee house and hiring hall where the houses' senior servants meet, gossip, and hire staff. References are checked here. Careers are ended here.
- **Why a crew might go there:** Want to get someone into a house as a servant? Want to know which footman drinks? This is where you ask.

### 🫖 The Saltmarsh Rooms
- **Where:** The lower Terraces, near the Span Hoist.
- **What it is:** A respectable boarding house for Terraces clerks, run by a widow who doesn't ask questions as long as the rent's on time. Half the Fixers in the Margin have rented a room here at some point.
- **Why a crew might go there:** A safe bed above the Span, if you can pass for respectable.

## 🤝 Places in the Margin

### 🍺 The Halfway House
- **Where:** Where the north bridges of the Span meet.
- **What it is:** The Margin's oldest tavern. Every fixer drinks here. The back room is where **the Brokers' Table** sits, and where the Margin Peace is kept.
- **Why a crew might go there:** To find a fixer, a buyer, a forger, or a job. To be seen to be respectable, by Margin standards.

### 🏛️ The Glass Market
- **Where:** The east side of the Span.
- **What it is:** The biggest market hall in the city, under a roof of cracked glass. Terraces goods, Works goods, off-world goods. Mostly legal.
- **Why a crew might go there:** Shopping, meetings, and the kind of crowd it's easy to disappear into.

### 🪝 Hook Street
- **Where:** The lowest gallery of the Span, wet with the Weep.
- **What it is:** Cheap bars, cheap rooms, Specialist dealers, gang armourers, and doctors who don't write anything down.
- **Why a crew might go there:** It's where the Specialist market lives.

### 🌉 The Long Bridge
- **Where:** Right across the Well, east to west.
- **What it is:** The only bridge that crosses the whole Well, half a mile of iron over nothing. Duels are fought on it at dawn. It's the only place on the Span where the Margin Peace doesn't hold, by long tradition, as long as both sides agreed to meet.
- **Why a crew might go there:** To settle something. Or to watch someone else settle something.

## 🔩 Places in the Works

### 💧 The Great Pumps
- **Where:** The eastern floor, at the bottom of Antioch's main pressure lines.
- **What it is:** Antioch's pumping station: a cathedral of iron, steam and noise, where the water from the rim is driven out to more than a hundred districts. The **Gauger-General** runs it, and every district Gauger answers to her.
- **Why a crew might go there:** Because it's the heart of everything Antioch holds over your district. And because every other house would like to see it fail, just once, just before the Assessor arrives.

### 🔥 The Kindling
- **Where:** The centre of the Well's floor.
- **What it is:** The great deep boilers that drink the heat at the bottom of the Well, and drive everything in the city. So hot that the Weep turns back to steam before it lands. The stokers' shrines line every boiler.
- **Why a crew might go there:** Sabotage. Or to hide something where nobody will look, because nobody can stand the heat long enough.

### 🥊 The Kettle
- **Where:** The northern floor, between three districts that all claim it.
- **What it is:** An old boiler hall turned fighting pit. Every Slack Day, champions from every district fight for chips, pride and their gang's name. The betting is run by the Sunk.
- **Why a crew might go there:** Kettle money. Meetings with other gangs on neutral ground. And everyone who matters in the Works comes to the big fights.

### 🪜 The Thousand Steps
- **Where:** Up the northern wall, from the floor to the Span.
- **What it is:** The great public stair, iron and stone, with landings, shrines, stalls and beggars. It's more than a thousand steps. Nobody's counted for a while.
- **Why a crew might go there:** It's the only way up that's free.

### 🏭 Slagside
- **Where:** The western floor.
- **What it is:** Varre's biggest foundry district, and home of the **Slagborn**. The slag heaps glow at night. The water is the worst in the Works.
- **Why a crew might go there:** Because Varre's crew lives here. Because the Slagborn want better water and your district has it.

### 🪦 Low Mereth
- **Where:** The southern floor, against the wall.
- **What it is:** The only district that never signed with another house after Mereth fell. It's had no house water for forty-six years, and it's still there. Nobody knows how. The people of Low Mereth don't say.
- **Why a crew might go there:** If there's a way to live without a house's water, it's here.

## 🛶 Places in the Sunk

### 🛶 Gutterlight
- **Where:** The biggest flooded hall in the upper Sunk.
- **What it is:** A floating market of barges lashed together, lit by lamps hung from the ceiling on long chains. Stolen goods, forged papers, and the city's only market in stolen Refinement.
- **Why a crew might go there:** The Refined market. Fences. Anything you can't buy anywhere else.

### ⛪ The Drowned Chapel
- **Where:** Down a flooded stair from Gutterlight.
- **What it is:** A Bonded hall, older than anything else in the city, half underwater. Carvings on the walls show people, boilers, and something round and glowing at the bottom of the Well. The lenders meet here. Nobody draws a blade inside.
- **Why a crew might go there:** To borrow chips. To pay them back. To beg for more time.

### 🚪 Mereth's Gate
- **Where:** The deep galleries, below the lamps.
- **What it is:** A great iron door in the rock, stamped with Mereth's crown, and welded shut from the outside. Nobody knows what's behind it. The ferrymen won't stop near it.
- **Why a crew might go there:** Somebody's always offering to pay a crew to open it.

### 🌀 The Sump
- **Where:** The lowest open water in the Sunk.
- **What it is:** A black lake in a cavern nobody's mapped. The bitterwater glows faintly here. The ferrymen go no further.
- **Why a crew might go there:** Don't.

## 🧊 Places Outside

### 🧊 The Ice Roads
- **Where:** Out across the Pale, from the eastern rim to the glaciers.
- **What it is:** Antioch's crawler routes: hundreds of miles of track across frozen stone, marked with iron posts and shelter huts. The **Shelter at Nine Mile** is the last place a hauler can turn back.
- **Why a crew might go there:** Somebody wants an ice crawler to go missing. Somebody else wants it to arrive.

### 🏔️ The Glaciers
- **Where:** Weeks out on the Pale.
- **What it is:** Where the ice comes from. Antioch's cutting camps work the edges. Haulers say there are old things frozen in the ice: machines, ships, and once, something that looked like a door.
- **Why a crew might go there:** Almost nobody does. But almost nobody isn't nobody.

| ⚖️ **Marshal Odile Corvane** | Warden of the Well. Reads every arrest report herself. Said never to have been wrong about anyone |
| 🌾 **Steward Benedek Aurel** | Head of Aurel. Big, warm, laughing. Knows every ration clerk by name. Less fun if you owe him |
| 🥀 **The Dowager Calyx** | Head of House Calyx. Nobody knows her name or her age. Attends every funeral in the Heights in person |
