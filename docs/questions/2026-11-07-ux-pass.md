## 1. Do traits add dice To Wound?

**Conflict between two rules files.**

- [dice_system.md](../../dice_system.md), the Combat Sequence diagram (line 139), lists **+ Trait** under both To Hit and To Wound.
- [character_creation.md](../../character_creation.md), Traits in combat: *"Combat traits (Brawler and Marksman) cover the To Hit roll. **No trait covers To Wound.** Damage comes from your gear."*

The app's dice counter currently follows character_creation.md: no trait on To Wound.

- **A.** No trait on To Wound. Remove "+ Trait" from the To Wound column of the diagram. *(Recommended: it matches the written rule and keeps damage coming from gear, which is why chips matter.)*
- **B.** Traits do add `+1` To Wound. Change character_creation.md, the Dice Modifiers page and the app's counter.

**Answer:**
A

✅ **Done.** Removed "+ Trait" from the To Wound column of the Combat Sequence diagram in dice_system.md. Character Creation, Dice Modifiers and the counter already said no trait.

## 2. Share material with chosen players only?

The old snippet feed could share a handout with **chosen players** (a secret for one character). Campaign material segments are shared with **the whole campaign** or nobody. A DM who wants to give one player a secret has no way to do it now.

- **A.** Keep it campaign-wide. Secrets for one player happen at the table.
- **B.** Each **segment** can be shared with everyone, or with chosen players.
- **C.** Each **section** can be DM only, Players (everyone), or chosen players. Most flexible, and a bit more to set up per section. *(Recommended if one-player secrets matter in your games.)*

**Answer:**
C

✅ **Done.** Each section is now **DM only**, **Players** or **Chosen**, with a checklist of the campaign's players. The API sends each player only their sections. A player never sees who else a chosen section went to, and a section just for them is labelled "Just for you". New and Updated markers and the unread dot are worked out per player, so one player's secret doesn't show up as a change for anyone else. Importing a backup turns Chosen sections into DM only, because the players belonged to the old campaign. API tests cover the allowed and refused cases.

## 9. Accessibility audit

The app was built to WCAG 2.2 AA (contrast, 44px tap targets, visible focus that isn't hidden by the bars, labelled controls, keyboard support for the bottom sheet), and checked by hand in a browser. No automated audit tool has been run.

- **A.** Add an automated check (axe-core in the local browser test run) and fix what it finds.
- **B.** Test on real phones with a screen reader (VoiceOver, TalkBack) first.
- **C.** Not needed now.

**Answer:**
A

✅ **Done.** Added an axe-core check in `test/browser/` (`npm install && npm test`, see Tests in the README). It walks 28 screens as a visitor, a DM and two players, in both themes, against WCAG 2.2 A and AA. It found and fixed:

- Unlabelled checkboxes in task lists on the Actions page (they're now named Done or Not done).
- Crew sheet tier buttons whose accessible name left out their visible text (WCAG 2.5.3).

It now passes with no violations. The walk also turned up a bug: the Chosen list missed players who joined after the DM opened the campaign. The editor now reloads the member list when a segment opens.
