## 3. How big is the Well?

The old lore made the Well 3 miles across and a mile deep. Your notes say vents can be over 10 km wide and some seem bottomless.

- **A.** The Well is about **12 km across**, its walls drop **2 km to the Floor**, and the Floor is a crust over a vent that goes on down (the Sunk is dug into that crust). Other wells are smaller, and **the Maw** (about 20 km, uninhabitable) is the biggest. The Span is now about 40 km round, and the Long Bridge is 12 km. *(applied)*
- **B.** Keep the Well small (about 5 km). The big vents are elsewhere in the Rim.

## 6. What the moons are mined for

Your notes say the three moons have varying industry, *"usually linked [to] the mining [of] the resources"*. I read that as mining on the moons themselves:

- **The Salt** (big, white): Crown mines, metal for chips and the Conservatory.
- **The Ember** (red): Varre's iron and rare-ore mines, the "Ember Lease". Antioch's fleet carries the ore down, which feeds Varre's grudge.
- **The Shard** (small, white): the Netwrights' station, and Corvane's penal mine.

- **A.** Keep this. *(applied)*
- **B.** You meant the moons' industries serve mining on the planet (for example, refining or shipping what the Rim digs up). Say how.
