The crater spans nearly 100km with many vents that can be over 10km in width, and some feeliung infintely deep. Tunnels conenct many of these vents, joining different layers which reflect those layers in society already defined. # Travel

Travel amongst the different vents is achieved in a variety of ways. For domestic travel within the rim, mechanized carts transport civilians. On the lower layers, these look more like minecarts powered by steam engines. Above, these are lightning fast mono-rails, still using steam power, but the way in which the steam is concealed makes them look light years beyond the look of those lower down.

Above the rim, air travel is popular. These are large galleons, similar to those of ancient terran sea fareres. Large solar masts, steam powered cannons, and large decks protected from the elements with greenhouse like domes. These domes are retractable. Notably there are liveried galleons, and beyond the rim pirates who have seized these ships, and frequent air battles amongst the rim and other locations where human populations survive.

There are also the great ice hallers. Massive machines that haul large blocks of ice across the surface to drop into the vents, producing the precious steam The Society needs to much. Likewise, this is the way in which water is generated for the populations, and is key for growing the foods available. There are no lakes, rivers or seas on the planet, so mining dense blue ice is required to keep the society working.
